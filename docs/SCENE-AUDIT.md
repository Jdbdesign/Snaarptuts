## Key Deviations To Remediate

1. **Missing `pressOn` pattern**: Almost every click was written as raw `moveCursorTo(selector)` followed by `click(selector)`, skipping the house pattern (`moveCursorTo -> highlight -> wait(150) -> click -> clearHighlight`).
2. **Missing `hoverOn` pattern**: Descriptive beats glided to buttons or cards without triggering `hover` or standard ring pauses.
3. **Missing post-action wait buffers**: When `setVar` changed pages, tabs, or modal states, the next beat queried the newly mounted elements immediately without the required `80ms` (DOM mount) or `250ms+` (layout transition) buffer.
4. **Missing standard step finish**: Every step currently ends with raw `hideCaption()` and `await tl.at(DURATION)`, lacking the house white fade-out (`finishWorkforce`).
5. **Typing helper misuse**: In Steps 6 and 17, `typeText` had its parameters inverted (`typeText(text, key)` instead of `typeText(key, text)`), which bypassed typing into `field:<key>`. `tl.fill(selector, key, text)` will be used instead.
