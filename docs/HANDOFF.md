# Handoff - read this first

## What this project is

Snaarp Tutorials are full-screen, auto-playing, step-by-step tutorials that teach people how to sign up for and use Snaarp products. Each one lives on its own page (`/tutorials/<product-id>`). The screen is a left step rail plus a "browser window" in the middle. Inside the window a fake cursor clicks, types and scrolls through **real HTML/CSS screens** (not a video, not screenshots), with short captions telling the viewer what to click.

First product: **Snaarp 360**. More products will follow, each with its own modules (steps).

**The owner is a product designer and does not write code.** Give short, plain-language summaries. Do not explain technical steps unless asked. When something must be done by hand, give clear numbered steps. Build **one step per round**, verify it, then stop and wait for feedback.

**Repository:** https://github.com/Jdbdesign/Snaarptuts (branch `main`). Source videos and reference images live in `ref/` and are intentionally not in git.

## Player: saved progress
Completed steps are saved in the browser. `lib/progress.ts` keeps one localStorage key, `snaarp-tutorials:progress:v1`, shaped `{ [tutorialSlug]: { completed: [stepId], updatedAt } }` (only slugs, step ids and timestamps). A step is saved the moment it plays to its end (the same moment the check appears; `progressStore.complete` is called from `TutorialPlayer.handleDone` and writes synchronously). `lib/useProgress.ts` (`useSyncExternalStore`) feeds the rail checks, every product's "n/total" counter (also in the product dropdown) and `TutorialPlayer`; counters are `invisible` (space kept) for the first hydration frame (`ready`). On load the JSON is validated and cleaned: bad JSON or shape gives empty progress, and unknown slugs or step ids are dropped silently (a new step simply shows unchecked). Every storage call is in try/catch; if storage is blocked or full the player keeps in-memory progress. A `storage` event listener keeps two tabs in sync. Replay no longer clears the checks. A quiet "Reset progress" button (`components/player/ResetProgress.tsx`, copy in `progress-data.ts`) sits at the bottom of the rail and clears everything after a confirm popover. **Step ids are now the saved identity of a step: never rename or reuse a step `id` in `tutorials/*.ts` (titles and order may change freely); a renamed id loses its saved check.**

Manual test of saved progress (no unit-test runner in the project): 1) finish a step, reload: the check and counter stay. 2) Open a second tab, finish a step of another product: the first tab's product dropdown counter updates without reload. 3) Run `localStorage.setItem("snaarp-tutorials:progress:v1", JSON.stringify({"snaarp-360":{completed:["choose-plan","no-such-step"]}}))` and reload: only "choose-plan" counts. 4) Set the key to `{not json` and reload: empty progress, no error. 5) Clear site data and reload: no checks. 6) Block storage (browser setting or an init script that makes `window.localStorage` throw): the player still plays and checks appear for the session. 7) Reset progress, Cancel keeps the checks, "Clear progress" removes them at once. The pure functions (`parseProgress`, `withCompleted`, `completedCount`) are in `lib/progress.ts` for when a test runner is added.
